Max's Sudoku

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Max's Sudoku collects no data. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no accounts, and the app asks for no permissions. This page explains what the app keeps, and where.

On your device

Your settings, the game in play, and a few puzzles made ahead of time are kept on your device, in the app's own storage. They never leave it. Puzzles are made on your device, and hints are worked out there too.

In your iCloud

If your device is signed in to iCloud, the games in your History sync through your own iCloud account, so they're on all your devices. For each game, your iCloud keeps:

  • the puzzle and its difficulty
  • when you started it and when you last played it
  • the kind of device you started it on: iPhone, iPad or iPhone Duo (never its name)
  • how long you've played it
  • every move you made (digits, notes, shortlists, colors, erasures and undos), each with the moment in the game it was made
  • which hints you looked at

That's all. Your games are kept in a private database in your iCloud account, which only your devices signed in to it can read. I can't see them, and they're never sent anywhere else. Apple stores them under Apple's own privacy policy, and they count, in a small way, toward your iCloud storage. A game takes a few hundred bytes.

To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for Sudoku in the Settings app, under your name › iCloud. To delete the games from iCloud, go to your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage, then Sudoku. Deleting the app deletes everything it kept on that device.

Buying Sudoku Pro

Sudoku Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment. The app only learns from the App Store whether Pro is owned, and nothing about you or how you paid.

Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight

When you ask Siri or Shortcuts for something in the app, the app answers with what's needed: the board, a hint, or your stats. Where Apple Intelligence is available, a hint may be reworded by its on-device model. Siri and Apple Intelligence handle your requests under Apple's privacy policy. Your games are listed in Spotlight's search index on your device, so you can find them there and ask Siri about them.

Apple Pencil

On iPad, digits written with Apple Pencil are read on the device. Your handwriting isn't kept or sent anywhere.

Sharing

Share Board makes a picture of the board. It goes only where you choose to send it.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here, with its date.
