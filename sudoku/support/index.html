Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to support@maxsgames.app. I read everything.

How do I restore Sudoku Pro on a new device?

Open ⋯ (More) › Upgrade to Pro and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. Pro also unlocks on its own on every iPhone and iPad signed in with that account.

How does History sync between my devices?

Through your own iCloud. Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on each device, and make sure iCloud is on for Sudoku (Settings › your name › iCloud). Games show up on your other devices when you open the app, and every 30 seconds while you play. You can start a game on your iPhone and pick it up on your iPad from History with Continue Solving.

My games aren't showing up on my other device.

Check both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, with iCloud on for Sudoku. Then open the app on the device you played on, and once more on the other. If a game was played on both devices at once, the copy that got further is kept.

Can my family use Sudoku Pro?

Yes. Sudoku Pro works with Family Sharing. Once Family Sharing is set up, members of your family can unlock it with Restore Purchases, or it unlocks on its own.

What does Sudoku Pro include?

Every game you play in History, with replays, analysis and your times at each difficulty, and every wallpaper. It's a one-time purchase, with no subscription. Without Pro, the app is complete, with unlimited games, hints, the Guide, and three wallpapers.

I played games before buying Pro. Are they gone?

No. Every game is kept from the start, so Pro shows them all.

Does Max's Sudoku collect my data?

No. Read the privacy policy.
